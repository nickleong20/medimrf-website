Automated Waste Sorting for Healthcare

Cutting waste disposal costs, mitigating human health and regulatory risk, and driving clinical sustainability.

MediMRF operates downstream at hospital loading bays, analyzing and sorting bagged municipal solid waste after clinical disposal to intercept misclassified items before municipal haul or expensive thermal treatment.

System Architecture

Engineered for Hospital Reality

Solving the clinical sorting problem at the facility perimeter rather than adding burden to patient care floors.

AI Computer Vision & Robotic Sorting

Automated optical inspection and precision sorting classify incoming Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) streams into recyclables, organics, and landfill fractions—while detecting and isolating improperly disposed Regulated Medical Waste (RMW), hazardous items, sharps, and clinical materials with high precision.

High-Precision Hazard Detection

Loading Dock Integration

Designed with a compact footprint engineered specifically to fit seamlessly into existing hospital loading bay docks. The unit adapts to standard facility roll carts, compactor layouts, and environmental services (EVS) workflows without requiring structural dock overhauls.

Minimal Spatial Footprint

Waste Diversion & Risk Mitigation

Redirects misclassified waste streams and prevents costly overclassification, significantly mitigating disposal expenses while dramatically accelerating waste diversion benchmarks and healthcare system ESG sustainability commitments.

Accelerated Diversion & ESG Goals
