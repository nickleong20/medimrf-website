Our Technological Innovation

Solving Hospital Waste at the Loading Dock

Hospitals misclassify tons of clean plastics as biohazard red bags. MediMRF corrects sorting upstream.

AI & Computer Vision Sorting

Trained classification algorithms rapidly differentiate rigid clean clinical plastics, recyclable packaging, and regulated hazardous streams with high precision.

Modular Dock Integration

Designed with a compact, skid-mounted footprint engineered to retrofit existing hospital loading bays, service elevators, and waste compactor corridors without structural overhauls.

Cost Reductions & ESG Impact

Redirecting misplaced benign waste from pricey autoclave/incineration streams ($0.30+/lb) into recycling saves hundreds of thousands annually while boosting healthcare ESG metrics.
