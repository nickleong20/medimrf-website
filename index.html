Automated Waste Sorting for Healthcare

Cutting waste disposal costs, mitigating human health and regulatory risk, and driving clinical sustainability.

MediMRF operates downstream at hospital loading bays, analyzing and sorting bagged municipal solid waste after clinical disposal to intercept misclassified items before municipal haul or expensive thermal treatment.

System Architecture

Post-Disposal Automated Intelligence

Built to handle complex hospital municipal streams without altering clinical staff routines upstairs.

AI & Computer Vision Sorting

Directly analyzes MSW streams to segregate recyclables, compostable materials, and landfill, while catching improperly disposed regulated medical waste (RMW), hazardous items, sharps, and pharmaceuticals with high precision.

High-Precision Hazard Detection

Compact Loading Dock Footprint

Engineered with a compact physical footprint specifically configured to fit inside existing hospital loading bays. It integrates smoothly into environmental service (EVS) cart flows without facility redesigns or capital-heavy overhauls.

Drop-in Bay Compatibility

Waste Diversion & Risk Mitigation

Redirects misclassified waste streams and prevents overclassification across entire hospital networks. Eliminates unnecessary high-cost hazardous hauling while accelerating institutional landfill diversion and sustainability goals.
