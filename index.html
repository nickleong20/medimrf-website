Automated Waste Sorting for Healthcare

Cutting waste disposal costs, mitigating human health and regulatory risk, and driving clinical sustainability.

MediMRF operates post-disposal at hospital loading docks. Our modular system leverages AI computer vision and intelligent sorting to separate municipal solid waste, catch hazardous misclassifications, and optimize hospital waste streams without altering internal clinical routines.

Engineered for Loading Docks

Post-Disposal Hospital Waste Intelligence

AI Computer Vision & Multi-Stream Sorting

Processes municipal solid waste (MSW) streams to systematically separate recyclables, organics, and landfill fractions, while actively identifying improperly disposed regulated medical waste (RMW), hazardous materials, and sharps with high precision.

High-Precision Hazard Detection

Seamless Loading Bay Footprint

Engineered with a compact footprint designed to fit into existing hospital loading bays and dock operations. Directly integrates into active environmental service (EVS) cart handling without requiring costly infrastructural overhauls.

Tailored to Existing EVS Workflows

Diversion Goals & Risk Mitigation

Redirects misclassified waste streams and eliminates the severe expense of overclassification. Helps hospitals significantly boost waste diversion goals, satisfy regulatory compliance, and mitigate health risks downstream.

Avoids Overclassification Penalties
